The Legend of Abyssia

Before the tides turned dark, the Sunken Kingdom of Abyssia thrived beneath the endless ocean. Eight great betta lineages ruled in harmony, their power bound to the Coral Crown — an ancient relic forged from living reef and infused with the ocean's primordial magic. The Crown maintained balance across all currents, from the sunlit shallows to the crushing depths of the hadal trenches.

But the Abyss King, once a noble guardian of the deep, gazed too long into the void beyond the ocean floor. The darkness whispered promises of power beyond the Crown's constraints. In a catastrophic act of betrayal, he shattered the Coral Crown into seven fragments and scattered them across the ocean, plunging Abyssia into chaos. The great currents failed, corruption seeped from the deepest trenches, and the ancient enemy creatures — long held at bay by the Crown's power — rose to claim the fractured kingdom.

Now, from every reef and every trench, betta warriors of every lineage answer the call. Halfmoons with their perfect symmetry, fierce Plakat brawlers, mystical Doubletails, ghostly Cambodians, mighty Giants, royal Crowntails, armored Dragonscales, and graceful Butterflies — all must set aside their differences and forge unlikely alliances. Schools of heroes must venture into corrupted waters, battle the Abyss King's forces, recover the Crown fragments, and restore the light to the dying ocean.

But the gods themselves have taken notice. Poseidon, Lord of Tides, tests the worthiest champions. Charybdis, The Devourer, hungers for the souls of heroes. And the Leviathan, Weaver of Currents, manipulates the tides of fate itself. Only those who prove themselves against all three divine powers can hope to reassemble the Coral Crown and face the Abyss King on his throne of devoured light.

The ocean remembers. The Crown calls. Will you answer?

The Eight Breeds

Eight distinct betta lineages, each carrying unique genetic traits recognized by the International Betta Congress (IBC). Choose your breed wisely — each brings different strengths to the battlefield.

Halfmoon

Balanced Perfection
Trait: Tidal Flow

Balanced perfection — a 180° caudal spread with full ray branching and flawless symmetry.

Halfmoons are the ideal show-standard betta, prized for their perfect 180-degree tail spread and balanced finnage. In the arenas of Abyssia, their symmetrical form translates to well-rounded combat ability — no fault in any attribute, no weakness to exploit.

+1 All Stats

Plakat

Short-Finned Brawler
Trait: Blood Frenzy

Short-finned brawlers — compact, aggressive, and bred for battle since the old fighting lines.

Plakats descend from the original fighting bloodlines — short, muscular fins built for speed and power. In IBC terms, their compact form avoids the fin-drag faults of longer breeds, channeling raw aggression into devastating strikes.

+4 STR+2 VIT+2 END

Doubletail

Twin-Lobed Mystic
Trait: Arcane Depths

Twin-lobed and mystical — a rare recessive breed channeling arcane currents through split caudal fins.

Doubletails carry the rare homozygous recessive gene that splits their caudal fin into two equal lobes. In Abyssia, this genetic rarity manifests as deep attunement to ocean magic — their twin fins act as dual conduits for arcane energy.

+3 INT+2 DEX+2 AGI+1 WIS

Cambodian

Pale Phantom
Trait: Phantom Scales

Pale and spectral — reduced pigment bettas from the frozen depths, nearly impossible to kill.

Cambodians carry the recessive reduced-pigment gene, giving them translucent, ghostly bodies. IBC judges note their clear body coloration as one of the rarest stable phenotypes — in combat, this translucency makes them extraordinarily difficult to target.

+3 VIT+2 END+2 WIS+1 STR

Giant

Oversized Predator
Trait: Reef Fury

Oversized reef predators — the enlarged chr8 locus lineage with devastating raw power.

Giants carry the rare enlarged body gene from chromosome 8, making them significantly larger than standard bettas. In IBC shows, maintaining perfect finnage at giant size is nearly impossible — but in battle, their sheer mass overwhelms any opponent.

+3 STR+2 AGI+1 VIT+1 END+1 TAC

Crowntail

Royal Defender
Trait: Royal Guard

Royal defenders — extended ray tips form a crown of spines, armoring them in living gold.

Crowntails display the dramatic extended-ray phenotype, where fin rays extend well beyond the webbing to form a crown-like silhouette. IBC standards demand even ray extension with no crossed tips — in Abyssia, these spined fins serve as both shield and symbol of royal lineage.

+3 END+2 VIT+1 STR+1 DEX+1 WIS

Dragonscale

Armored Speedster
Trait: Thermal Dash

Armored speedsters — thick metallic iridescent scales over a blazing orange body.

Dragonscales exhibit the highly coveted thick iridescent overlay that covers their body like armor plating. IBC judges score them on full coverage without gaps. In combat, their metallic armor deflects glancing blows while their compact build enables lightning dashes.

+3 AGI+2 STR+2 DEX+1 END

Butterfly

Graceful Healer
Trait: Healing Tide

Graceful healers — distinctive banded fin patterns that pulse with restorative energy.

Butterflies display the striking bi-color band pattern across their fins, with sharp divisions between colors that IBC judges scrutinize for clean edges. In Abyssia, these banded fins pulse with healing bioluminescence — the sharper the color bands, the more potent the restorative magic.

+3 WIS+2 INT+1 VIT+1 DEX+1 AGI

Betta Genetics & IBC Standards

Each breed carries unique genetic traits recognized by the International Betta Congress. Understanding these genetics is key to building the perfect Warlord team.

Dominant Trait

The 180° Spread Gene

The Halfmoon's perfect 180-degree caudal spread is a polygenic trait requiring multiple alleles for full expression. IBC judges use a protractor to measure — anything less than 180° is considered a fault. In Abyssia, this genetic perfection translates to balanced combat stats with no exploitable weakness.

Fighting Line

Short-Fin Phenotype

Plakats preserve the ancestral short-fin morphology of wild Betta splendens. While show bettas were selectively bred for flowing fins, Plakats maintain the compact, powerful build of the original fighting lines. Their reduced fin area means less drag and faster strikes — pure combat efficiency.

Recessive Gene

Homozygous Double Caudal

The Doubletail phenotype requires two copies of the recessive dt allele (homozygous dt/dt). This splits the caudal peduncle, producing twin tail lobes and an exceptionally wide dorsal fin. Heterozygous carriers (dt/+) show no visible split but can produce Doubletail offspring.

Recessive Pigment

Reduced Pigmentation

Cambodians carry the recessive c allele that suppresses body pigmentation while allowing fin color to remain. This creates their distinctive pale, translucent body that seems to glow in deep water. The clear flesh layer is one of the most difficult stable phenotypes to breed and maintain across generations.

Chromosome 8

Giant Growth Factor

The Giant betta's enlarged body size traces to a growth factor gene on chromosome 8. Unlike other breeds that max out around 6cm, Giants can reach 10cm or more. This requires careful selective breeding — maintaining both size AND proper finnage proportions. In IBC competition, Giant class bettas are judged separately.

Ray Extension

Extended Ray Phenotype

Crowntails express a gene that causes fin rays to grow 33% or more beyond the webbing, creating the distinctive crown-like appearance. IBC standards require at least 33% ray extension with no crossed or curled tips. The "webbing reduction" creates natural defensive spines in combat.

Iridescent Layer

Metallic Scale Overlay

Dragonscales develop a thick layer of iridescent guanine crystals over their scales, creating a metallic, armor-like appearance. IBC judges score for "full mask" coverage — any gaps in the metallic overlay are considered faults. Over generations, this armor can cause diamond eye (scale overgrowth), a known tradeoff in breeding.

Color Banding

Bi-Color Band Pattern

Butterflies express a unique color distribution gene that creates distinct bands of color across their fins, typically with a clear outer band and a colored inner band. IBC standards demand sharp, clean transitions between color zones — blurred or uneven bands are marked as faults. The banding pattern is believed to interact with bioluminescent proteins.

Hero Attributes

Eight core attributes define every Warlord's combat capabilities. Each breed and class modifies these stats differently.

Strength

STR

Physical power and melee damage. Determines how hard your strikes hit.

Vitality

VIT

Max HP and survivability. More vitality means more hits you can take.

Endurance

END

Defense and damage reduction. Reduces incoming physical and magical damage.

Intellect

INT

Magic damage and spell power. Amplifies arcane and elemental abilities.

Wisdom

WIS

Healing power and magic defense. Boosts restorative abilities and arcane resistance.

Dexterity

DEX

Critical hit chance. Higher dexterity means more devastating critical strikes.

Agility

AGI

Speed and evasion. Determines turn order and chance to dodge attacks.

Tactics

TAC

Turn order and combo efficiency. Enables better tactical decisions in battle.

Resources & Economy

Gather resources from the ocean to forge equipment, brew potions, and strengthen your school of warriors.

Pearls

The primary currency of Abyssia. Earned from battles, trading, and exploration. Used to buy equipment, rest at inns, and upgrade gear.

Coral

Harvested from coral groves. Used in crafting weapons and armor. The building material of the deep.

Shells

Mined from shell deposits deep in the ocean floor. Essential for forging heavy armor and shields. The ore of the deep.

Algae

Gathered from kelp forests and anemone gardens. Used in brewing potions and creating magical enchantments.

Crystals

Rare minerals found in crystal grottos and volcanic vents. Required for high-tier enchantments and legendary equipment.
